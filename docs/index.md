# TellerFlow

[![Java CI](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2103T-F08-4/tp/actions/workflows/gradle.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/AY2627S1-CS2103T-F08-4/tp/actions/workflows/gradle.yml)

TellerFlow helps bank tellers maintain client profiles from the keyboard. Enter commands to manage records, then use the graphical client list and details panel to review the information needed for the next interaction.

![TellerFlow client list and details panel, including the separate Tags section](images/Ui.png)

*Example profiles contain fictional client data.*

Create a profile with a reference and name, and fill in contact details, card information, credit score, debt, tags, or a service note as they become available. Find clients by name, phone number, or card number, and sort the displayed list by name. Successful client changes are saved automatically on your computer.
